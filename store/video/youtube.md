## Description

Shopping abroad, booking a trip or browsing a foreign store? DirectChange shows the live equivalent of every foreign price, right next to it, in your own currency.

¥24,000 ≈ €137. No copy-paste, no calculator, no tab switching.

✦ Hover a conversion to see the exact rate and when it was updated

✦ Detects the currency of the site you're on
✦ Available in 36 languages
✦ Free, open source, no account, no tracking
