Questions about DirectChange

What is DirectChange?

DirectChange is a free, open-source browser extension that converts prices into your own currency as you browse. It puts a small tag with the converted amount right after every foreign price on a web page, at today's live rate, and adds a quick converter to your browser toolbar.

Is DirectChange free?

Yes. DirectChange is free, with no paid plan, no ads and no account. It's open source under the MIT License, so anyone can read the code on GitHub.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and other Chromium browsers, through the Chrome Web Store, and Firefox on desktop and Android, through Firefox Add-ons. Safari isn't supported yet.

Where do the exchange rates come from?

From Coinbase's public exchange-rate API, refreshed every 10 minutes, with open.er-api.com as a daily fallback. It covers more than 150 currencies. Hover a tag to see the exact rate, when it was fetched and its source.

How does it know which currency a site uses?

A “$” isn't always US dollars. DirectChange reads the currency the shop declares in its product data, then the country domain (.ca, .com.au, .jp…), then the page language. Explicit symbols like € or C$ and codes like USD are never reinterpreted.

Can it include my card's foreign transaction fee?

Yes. Enter your card's fee abroad in the popup, for example 2%, and every tag shows what the price really costs you, fee included. The card that opens on hover shows the breakdown.

Does DirectChange collect my data?

No. The extension reads prices on your device and never sends page content, URLs or browsing history anywhere. Its only network requests fetch exchange rates, and they say nothing about you.
