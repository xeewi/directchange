Add to Chrome, it's free View the source

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and other Chromium browsers.

Small, quiet, and right where you look

It does one thing: tell you what a price means to you, without leaving the page.

⚡

Live rates

Near real-time rates for 150+ currencies, refreshed every 10 minutes. Hover any badge to see the exact rate and when it was fetched.

🧭

Knows where the site is

A “$” on a Canadian site is Canadian dollars. A “¥” on a Chinese site is yuan. DirectChange reads the domain, the page language and the shop's own data.

🔎

Reads prices like you do

฿14,000, 14 000 bahts, 1.234,56 €, USD 45, C$20, Rs 900… It even handles prices split across the page's HTML.

🎨

Blends into any site

A soft pill that adapts to light and dark pages. It's isolated from the site's styles, so it never breaks a layout.

🌍

Speaks 36 languages

Follows your browser by default, or pick a language in the settings. Right-to-left languages included.

🔒

Private by design

No account, no analytics. The page is read on your device and never sent anywhere.

Set it once, forget it

  1. Install

    Add DirectChange to your browser. It takes ten seconds.

  2. Pick your currency

    Euro, dollar, yen, baht… any of 150+ currencies. It defaults to your region's currency.

  3. Browse as usual

    Foreign prices get a small badge with their value in your currency, wherever you are on the web.

Your browsing stays yours

DirectChange collects nothing. The only requests it makes fetch exchange rates, and they say nothing about you or the pages you visit. The code is open source, so you can check. Read the privacy policy.
