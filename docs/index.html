DirectChange adds the live equivalent of every foreign price right after it, on any website. No more mental math, no more tab-switching to a converter.

On any page, right where you look

A trip wishlist in six currencies. Every price gets a small tag with its value in yours.

Any currency, anywhere

Yen, rupees, pesos or rand: DirectChange reads 150+ currencies and shows them in yours.

Small, quiet, and right where you look

It does one thing: tell you what a price means to you, without leaving the page.

Live rates

Near real-time rates for 150+ currencies, refreshed every 10 minutes. Hover any badge to see the exact rate and when it was fetched.

Knows where the site is

A “$” on a Canadian site is Canadian dollars. A “¥” on a Chinese site is yuan. DirectChange reads the domain, the page language, the shop's own data and the prices it shows.

Reads prices like you do

¥24,000, 1.234,56 €, MX$2,490, USD 45, 14 000 bahts, Rs 900… It even handles prices split across the page's HTML.

Blends into any site

A small tag that adapts to light and dark pages. It's isolated from the site's styles, so it never breaks a layout.

Speaks 36 languages

Follows your browser by default, or pick a language in the settings. Right-to-left languages included.

Private by design

No account, no analytics. The page is read on your device and never sent anywhere.

Set it once, forget it

  1. Install

    Add DirectChange to your browser. It takes ten seconds.

  2. Pick your currency

    Euro, dollar, yen, rupee… any of 150+ currencies. It defaults to your region's currency.

  3. Browse as usual

    Foreign prices get a small badge with their value in your currency, wherever you are on the web.
